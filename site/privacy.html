Full privacy notice (English)

Never send us a recovery phrase, private key, PIN or passkey credential. Support and email verification do not need them. A verified email cannot restore lost Bitcoin wallet keys.

Wallet and payment information

GetYourSats is intended as a self-custodial wallet built using the Breez Spark SDK. Wallet signing keys and recovery information are handled by the app and your chosen device/passkey recovery mechanism. Spark operators and related Lightning/payment services necessarily process technical wallet identities, payment requests and settlement data to provide network functionality. Bitcoin on-chain transactions are publicly visible.

Device and passkeys

Your device or password manager manages passkey authentication. GetYourSats does not need your biometric image or operating-system unlock PIN. A manually created recovery phrase should remain private and offline; the wallet may retain local wallet state for reconnection. Clearing browser or PWA storage may compromise access if you have no independent backup.

Optional email verification

If the optional verification service is activated and you use it, the service processes your email address, a signed wallet-identity challenge, short-lived verification codes and the resulting verified email-to-public-wallet link. Verification codes are designed to expire after 10 minutes and challenges after five minutes. Resend may deliver verification emails; Upstash may store server-side account and verification records. Your email may remain linked to the wallet until removal is completed through a supported account process. We do not treat a browser-local email setting as proof of ownership.

Service providers and operation

Vercel provides the website and deployment platform. Breez and Spark network services support wallet operations. If enabled, a third-party fiat on-ramp manages its own purchase flow, eligibility and KYC under its separate terms; GetYourSats does not currently represent that a purchase can be completed in the app.

Support and deletion

If you contact us, we process the information you submit in the support conversation. For privacy inquiries, questions about stored account information or requests to unlink an email, contact the private contact form. A request to remove server-held data may require proof of wallet identity and will not erase transactions from public Bitcoin networks or third-party records controlled by other services.

Updates

We may revise this notice as additional services become operational. Published product claims should reflect the actual deployed wallet and verified integrations. Last updated: 24 October 2026.
