This notice explains the types of information involved in using the GetYourSats Bitcoin and Lightning wallet, including what may remain on your device and what reaches service providers.

Last updated: October 23, 2026 · Public beta

Beta notice. GetYourSats is under active development and security validation. This page is a transparent summary of current product behavior, not a claim that the wallet or its recovery mechanisms have passed an independent audit. Do not rely on the beta to safeguard significant funds.

01 / Wallet information on your device

When you set up or restore a wallet, the app handles sensitive wallet key material and may store information needed to reconnect. Depending on your device and setup method, that can involve passkeys, native secure storage, or browser storage. The protections and recovery options are not identical on every platform.

The app can also save wallet preferences, recovery-method metadata, contacts and transaction-related information locally. Never share a recovery phrase, passkey secret or private key with support. An email address by itself cannot restore a self-custodial wallet.

02 / Bitcoin, Lightning and infrastructure

To connect and transact, the wallet uses the Breez SDK and services associated with Spark, Lightning and Bitcoin, alongside hosting infrastructure operated by Vercel. Wallet addresses, payment amounts, transaction identifiers and related technical requests may be processed by the applicable networks and service providers.

Like other internet services, infrastructure providers may receive network metadata such as IP addresses, request times, browser or device characteristics, and diagnostic events. Bitcoin transactions may become permanently visible on a public blockchain. The privacy practices of third-party providers are governed by their own notices.

03 / When you contact us

If you write to getyoursats@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include so that we can investigate and reply. Email delivery and mailbox storage also involve the email provider.

Please send only non-sensitive troubleshooting information, such as your device type, approximate time of an error and an error message with secret values removed. Do not email private keys, seed words, credentials or full recovery screenshots.

04 / Storage, records and retention

Some settings and wallet metadata remain in local device or browser storage until cleared, replaced or removed by the relevant wallet flow. Clearing an app or browser can make locally stored information unavailable; do not assume doing so is safe before verifying your recovery method.

Network transaction records may not be erasable. Support correspondence and provider diagnostic records can follow the retention practices of the relevant providers. We have not published a universal retention period for all provider-side data.

05 / Your choices and requests

You can decide whether to create a wallet, submit a payment, use optional recovery features, or contact support. You may also ask us about information provided directly to GetYourSats through support and request correction or deletion where applicable. We cannot erase public blockchain history or records controlled independently by third parties.

Before resetting, uninstalling or changing devices, verify that your current wallet can be restored. The beta does not promise recovery from an email address alone.

06 / Questions and changes

Privacy questions can be sent to getyoursats@gmail.com. As beta functionality, integrations and policies change, we will revise this notice and update the date shown above.

For wallet troubleshooting and safety instructions, see our Support page.
